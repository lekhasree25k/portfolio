About Me

ECE student with a systems mindset — curious about what breaks and how to prevent it.

I'm a third-year Electronics & Communication Engineering student at Rajalakshmi Institute of Technology, Chennai. My background is hardware and embedded systems — but what always fascinated me was: how do software systems stay reliable at scale? That question pulled me toward Cloud and DevOps.
